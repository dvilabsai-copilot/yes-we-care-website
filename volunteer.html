HOW IT WORKS

Five steps to support

1

Employee interest

Employees who wish to participate can register their interest in offering or receiving eligible elder care assistance.

2

Give support

Participating employees may volunteer for suitable, agreed help with appointments, essential errands or agreed travel for colleagues' elderly family members.

3

Request support

Employees who need help with their elderly parents or family members can submit a request through the designated process.

4

Manual coordination

Subject to availability, suitability, consent and organizational guidelines, suitable volunteers may be connected with employees requesting support.

5

Professional assistance

If no suitable volunteer is available, the request falls outside the volunteer program or professional care is needed, separately arranged paid support may be discussed.

PROGRAM PRINCIPLES

Responsible and respectful support

  • Voluntary participation
  • Consent and organizational guidelines
  • Matching subject to suitability and availability
  • Paid professional assistance offered separately when appropriate
