Frequently Asked Questions

What is Yes We Care Hospital & Home Assist Pvt. Ltd.?

Yes We Care Hospital & Home Assist Pvt. Ltd. is focused on healthcare concierge services, elder care assistance, hospital coordination, home assistance arrangements, travel companionship and pre-operative and post-operative support coordination.

Do you provide hospital assistance?

We help coordinate hospital-related requirements, including doctor appointments, hospital visits, admission and discharge arrangements and related services, depending on individual needs and availability.

Can you help arrange care for elderly parents while I am at work?

Yes. You can contact us to discuss your parents' requirements. We can explore suitable assistance options based on the requested service, location, schedule and availability.

Do you provide post-operative care?

We help coordinate suitable post-operative services, including home nursing, physiotherapy and follow-up arrangements, subject to availability and clinical needs.

Can you arrange a companion for a hospital visit or railway station journey?

You can contact us to discuss travel companion requirements, including accompaniment to hospitals, doctor appointments, diagnostic centres and agreed railway station journeys.

Do you offer free services?

Services are paid unless otherwise agreed. Eligible employee volunteer support depends on programme rules and volunteer availability.

Are services available every day?

Availability depends on the location, service type, schedule and availability of suitable personnel. Please contact us to confirm arrangements for your specific requirements.

Do you provide medical treatment?

Our primary focus is healthcare coordination and assistance arrangements. Medical consultations, diagnosis, prescriptions and clinical treatment must be provided by appropriately qualified and authorized healthcare professionals.

How can my company partner with YWC?

Contact us to explore employee wellness, elder care and volunteering initiatives tailored to your workplace.

How do I request assistance?

Call us at 9952040006 or complete the website enquiry form to discuss your requirements, availability and applicable charges.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry is only a request to discuss assistance. Service confirmation depends on the agreed scope, applicable charges, location and availability.

Still have questions?

Share your requirements and our team can discuss suitable assistance options.
